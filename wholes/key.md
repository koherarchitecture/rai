# a key

One key from your keyring or pocket. An example of a set written in Markdown: a title, a numbered list of questions, and an optional stem under each. Any other line, like this one, is a note and is ignored.

1. What is it made of, and what colour is it?
   - stem: It is made of
2. How long is it, against your finger?
   - stem: It is about as long as
3. What does its head look like?
   - stem: Its head is
4. Does it have any mark, number or scratch? Where?
   - stem: It has
5. What does it open?
   - stem: It opens
6. Where is it right now?
   - stem: It is
